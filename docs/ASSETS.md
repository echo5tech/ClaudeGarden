# Art direction and asset provenance

`apps/web/public/art/kitchen-garden.png` is an original decorative illustration generated with the built-in image generation tool for this rebuild. It is not a photograph of a user’s actual garden. The UI labels it as inspiration; garden previews are schematic overviews, not literal plant coordinates.

Final prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a premium editorial botanical illustration for the hero of a polished gardening app called WeGarden. Art only, no UI, no text, no letters, no logos. Wide landscape 1536x1024. A charming small kitchen garden seen in elegant slightly elevated three-quarter perspective: two terracotta-edged wooden raised beds lush with curled kale, leafy herbs, tomato vines with small red tomatoes, a slim natural wood trellis, a sage watering can. Sophisticated hand-painted gouache and fine colored-pencil texture on warm pale cream paper (#f4f1e5). Muted forest green, sage, olive, ochre, a little terracotta. Intentional mature magazine illustration, refined organic shapes, beautiful leaves, calm morning shadows. Garden occupies right two-thirds; mostly pale cream background on left with very faint grass details. No people, no cute faces, no photorealism, no words. This image is a decorative app asset.

UI icons use the existing Lucide dependency. Plant cards use schematic icons so catalog entries do not imply an unverified botanical photograph. Fonts use Next.js font loading (Geist and Lora).
